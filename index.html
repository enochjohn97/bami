How it works

Our Business Process

From acquiring fertile land to distributing premium palm oil worldwide — six disciplined stages, refined over years of estate management.

01

Land Preparation

We acquire and prepare fertile lands ideal for oil palm cultivation.

02

Planting

High-quality Bami palm seedlings are planted at optimum density.

03

Cultivation

We apply best agronomic practices, including fertilization and regular maintenance.

04

Harvesting

Fresh fruit bunches are harvested at peak ripeness for maximum oil content.

05

Processing

FFB are processed in our mills to extract crude palm oil (CPO) and by-products.

06

Distribution

We distribute premium palm oil and products to local and international markets.

Our Palm Variety

Bami

We cultivate the premium Bami oil palm hybrid, known for its high oil yield, disease resistance, and adaptability.

  • Higher oil-to-bunch ratio
  • Faster growth rate
  • High yield potential
  • Long economic lifespan

The Oil Palm Growth Journey

A single palm's productive life, from nursery seedling to a quarter-century of sustained yield.

Nursery Stage

1–3 YEARS

Establishment Stage

Palms are planted in the field and begin to establish.

3–4 YEARS

Vegetative Growth

4–5 YEARS
